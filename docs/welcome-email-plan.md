# Welcome email plan (draft only)

Do not send these emails and do not connect an email service until Cole approves a provider and the copy. Addresses collected on the site are stored only. This is a plan for later.

All messages should be short, from a recognizable sender (for example Cole / NoSpamCalls), include a way to opt out, and should not promise that spam will stop or that a Scam Likely label will be cleared.

## 1. App waitlist welcome

Subject: You’re on the NoSpamCalls app list — no charge today

We saved your email for the NoSpamCalls app waitlist.

The website is already free: look up a number, report it, and use the two-minute protection checkup.

If we launch an app and charge for it, people on this list will be offered founding-member pricing before the public price. We will tell you the amount first. Joining this list does not charge you, and we do not have a payment method on file.

We’ll write again when there is a real update.

## 2. Protection-plan email

Subject: Your NoSpamCalls protection plan

You asked us to save your spam-protection plan (score: [score] of 6).

Here are the next free steps we recommended: [insert the personalized checklist].

You can print the plan again at https://nospamcalls.vercel.app/protect

If you also joined the app waitlist, see the note above: no payment now.

## 3. Number-alert confirmation

Subject: We’ll watch [national number] on NoSpamCalls

You asked for an email if [national number] is reported again on NoSpamCalls.

We will only email you about this number. Community reports are user opinions, not a verified identification of the caller.

## 4. Number-alert notice (when a later report is filed)

Subject: New community report for [national number]

Someone filed another community report for [national number] on NoSpamCalls. You can read it here: [lookup URL]

These are user-submitted opinions, not verified facts.

## 5. Business Scam Likely check received

Subject: We received your Scam Likely check request

We saved your request for [business name] / [number].

What we can say today: [count] community reports on NoSpamCalls, or none yet. Our log is not what carriers or caller-ID apps use. We cannot promise to clear a label.

Useful official next step: ask the carrier that originates your calls about STIR/SHAKEN and any dispute process, and read https://nospamcalls.vercel.app/scam-likely

Your email is also on the business waitlist for a possible reputation service.

## 6. Pre-launch warmup (app list only, when a launch date exists)

Two short emails, then a launch ask:

- Three weeks out: one paragraph about what is actually ready. No hard sell.
- One week out: one paragraph and a question (reply with the feature you care about most).
- Launch: state whether the app is free, paid, or not ready; if paid, name the founding-member price and that they can decline; one link. Follow up after 72 hours only to people who did not click, if we have that capability.

## Provider notes (for later approval)

A later email setup should be a provider Cole already uses or a free/low-cost transactional tool he approves. Do not sign up for a paid plan without asking. Until then, keep collecting addresses and do not send mail.
